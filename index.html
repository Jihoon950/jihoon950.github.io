01 / Current research · AI-and-RAN

Sharing GPUs with the RAN in mind.

RAN-aware routing of AI inference between shared and disjoint GPU execution contexts. On an NVIDIA H100 testbed, I study how changing RAN workloads affect GPU contention and how adaptive sharing can improve AI throughput while respecting PHY timing constraints.

AI-and-RANGPU sharingNVIDIA H100
View the ongoing manuscript ↗

02 / Semantic communication

Protecting meaning, beyond bits.

Hierarchical token-to-bit mapping, unequal power allocation, and model-assisted recovery for reliable semantic communication. I also study how channel-posterior information can guide masked language models during token reconstruction.

Token communicationSemantic fidelityLanguage models
View submitted manuscripts ↗

03 / Connected mobility

Coordinating vehicles over the air.

Joint communication and control for vehicle platoons. AirCons exploits wireless signal superposition to share aggregate state information without individual channel-state feedback.

V2XDistributed controlOver-the-air computation
Read the TWC paper ↗

04 / 6G air interface

Keeping beams aligned in motion.

Position-aware MAC design and stochastic-geometry analysis of beam misalignment, with a focus on access probability, beamwidth, and the trade-off between alignment overhead and throughput.

A researcher
in motion.

I am a Ph.D. student at Yonsei University, advised by Prof. Seong-Lyun Kim in the Robotic and Mobile Networks Laboratory. I combine analytical modeling, algorithm design, and experimental systems to study communication under mobility and uncertainty. My current work investigates how RAN workload information can guide GPU sharing between radio processing and AI inference.
